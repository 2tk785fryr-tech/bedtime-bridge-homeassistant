How it fits together

Your phone plans. Your house acts.

📅

The app

Reads your shifts from your calendar and plans each morning: alarms, leave-by, wind-down.

⌂

The integration

Sets the alarms at bedtime and turns helpers on and off as you snooze, stop, nap and wind down.

💡

Your automations

React to those helpers with your lights, curtains and speakers, using the blueprints below.

Before you start

What you need

  • Home Assistant 2025.1 or later.
  • The Bedtime Bridge app on your iPhone, with Home Assistant chosen as your home.
  • The Home Assistant Companion app on the same iPhone, for notifications and your location.
By hand

Copy custom_components/bedtime_bridge into your config/custom_components/ folder, then restart.

1 · Install

From the Marketplace

Open Marketplace → ⋮ (top right) → Custom repositories.

Repository: https://github.com/2tk785fryr-tech/bedtime-bridge-homeassistant, Type: Integration → Add.

Search for Bedtime Bridge → Download, then restart Home Assistant.

2 · Add and pair

Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → Bedtime Bridge, and pick yourself (your person or phone).

In the app: Settings → Home Assistant → Pair. The app and the integration exchange a code; nothing to type.

Everything else in the integration's options is optional: add what you want, as below.

3 · Helpers

The switches your automations watch.

Make the ones you want in Settings → Devices & services → Helpers → Create helper → Toggle, then pick each in the integration's options (Bedtime Bridge → Configure).

4 · Blueprints

Tap to import. Pick your own devices.

Each blueprint is generic: nothing is filled in for you. Import it, create an automation from it, and choose your lights, speakers and sensors.

5 · Check it works

Three quick checks

  • Devices & services → Bedtime Bridge shows its sensors: next alarm, last plan, backstop times, snoozing.
  • The app's Settings → Home Assistant says Connected.
  • Tap Preview tomorrow in the app: the last plan sensor updates.
Troubleshooting

If something's off

“Not connected”

Check Home Assistant is reachable from your phone (try the Companion app), then pair again.

Alarms weren't set at bedtime

The missing plan blueprint warns you. Check the integration's last plan sensor.

A helper never turns on

Make sure it's picked in the integration's options.

Moving from an older install

No need to set it up again: add this repository, remove the old Bedtime Bridge entry, download from here, restart. Your pairing and settings stay.
